Hi! I’m Ellie.

I’m a PhD Candidate at Duke University studying computer science under the mentorship of David Carlson. I am also a filmmaker with a special interest in documentaries.

On the research side, I use generative AI, probabilistic machine learning, and physics to better inform climate adaptation. In particular, I develop new methods for spatiotemporal modeling and forecasting of urban heat and air pollution. I am especially interested in solving problems of sparse, noisy data in order to provide access to weather data for everyone, in order to mitigate exposure risks.

On the film side, I make documentaries in order to learn what draws people together: from music to board games to conspiracy theories. I am also an avid screenwriter; my work recently won at Duke Independent Film Festival (DIFF 2026).
